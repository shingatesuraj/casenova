About CaseNova

Designing spaces with purpose, passion and precision.

At Case Nova, design begins with intention — never decoration for its own sake. We shape homes around Purpose, Passion, and Precision, turning everyday spaces into ones that feel considered, calm, and unmistakably yours. From the first layout to the smallest material choice, nothing is left to chance.

This is design meant to be lived in, not just looked at — crafted for homeowners across India and beyond who want spaces that truly breathe.
